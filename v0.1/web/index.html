    OpenStreetMap supplements

    Contains information from OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL). Used for pharmacies, supermarkets, GPs, bus stops, address autocomplete (~414 k Berlin addresses), address history, and the Newcomer-lens buckets (international food, coworking / Wi-Fi cafés, English-speaking clinics, German-language schools, public libraries, Packstationen, Wochenmärkte, bars & clubs). Delivered via a weekly Berlin extract from Geofabrik (see their technical policy) — same ODbL terms, refreshed Sundays 03:00 CET.

    Map tiles & library

    Basemap tiles © CARTO and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Interactive maps rendered with Leaflet (BSD 2-clause).

    AI narratives

    Card insights, address-history summaries, and plain-English explanations are generated by open-weight language models: Cloudflare Workers AI (@cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct-fast) for the history endpoint, and a self-hosted llama.cpp server running Qwen 2.5 1.5B Instruct (Q4_K_M) for card insights & explanations. Street name and house number are stripped from prompts before sending — the model receives anonymised structured facts only.
